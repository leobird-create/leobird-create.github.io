The Green Mile

Released: 1999 | Director: Frank Darabont | Studio: Castle Rock Entertainment

The Green Mile (My Favourite)

Released: 1999 | Director: Frank Darabont | Studio: Castle Rock Entertainment

This is my favourite movie because of the deep emotional impact and the way it highlights the flaws in the justice system. The character of John Coffey is truly unforgettable.

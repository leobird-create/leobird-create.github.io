The Green Mile

"The Green Mile" is set in a Louisiana prison during the Great Depression. The story follows Paul Edgecomb, a death row corrections officer, and his encounter with John Coffey, a gentle giant convicted of murdering two young girls. Paul soon discovers that Coffey possesses a miraculous healing power, forcing him to question everything he believes about justice and humanity.

I love "The Green Mile" because it is a crime based movie and it had a significant impact on my perspective of the world. In the end when prison guards were crying, before executing John, that scene hit me so hard. Michael Clarke Duncan as John did one amazing job on screen. It also highlights a real issue in the justice system in modern society. The movie is a masterpiece and I would highly recommend it to everyone.

Characters & Facts

  • Paul Edgecomb - He is the protagonist and narrator of the film, he is also the head guard on death row.
  • John Coffey - He is 6'8", superhuman and acts like a child.
  • Brutus "Brutal" Howell - Despite his scary nickname, he is the kindest guard on the crew.
  • Percy Wetmore - He is a cruel guard who uses his political connections to avoid getting fired.
